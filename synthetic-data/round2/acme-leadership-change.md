# Acme Corp — Leadership Change

**Date:** 2026-07-10
**Source:** Acme press release / LinkedIn announcement

## What changed

Sarah Chen has stepped down as Chief Technology Officer effective July 1,
2026, to become Acme's Chief Product Officer as part of a broader
reorganization. **Sarah is no longer the CTO.**

Acme has named **Marcus Webb** (previously VP of Engineering) as the new
**Chief Technology Officer**, effective the same date.

## Why this matters

Any prior notes that treat Sarah Chen as the primary technical decision
maker / CTO are now out of date. Marcus Webb should be treated as the
current CTO and primary technical sponsor for renewal conversations going
forward.

Sarah remains an important relationship (she was the original champion and
is now CPO, a still-relevant stakeholder for product roadmap conversations),
but she is **not** the current CTO, and renewal outreach that assumes she
holds that title will look out of touch.
