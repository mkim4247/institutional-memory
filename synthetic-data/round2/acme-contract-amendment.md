**Effective date:** 2026-06-15

## What changed

This amendment was signed as a short-term bridge ahead of the full renewal
negotiation (the original contract's term technically lapsed March 31, 2026,
and this amendment retroactively extends coverage on a month-to-month basis
while renewal terms are finalized).

- **New term:** Month-to-month, effective 2026-04-01, until a full renewal
  order is signed
- **Pricing during bridge period:** Same as prior term ($350,000/yr base +
  $70,000/yr analytics add-on), no discount adjustment yet
- **The 10% multi-year discount from the original contract does NOT apply**
  during this bridge period — it was tied to the original 24-month term only
- **Support tier:** Unchanged (Premium, 4-hour SLA)

## Why this matters for the renewal proposal

Acme is currently on a month-to-month bridge, not a fresh multi-year term.
Any renewal proposal should:
1. Acknowledge that pricing has effectively been running at full (non-discounted)
   rate since April 2026.
2. Address the analytics tier upgrade need (see new support ticket
   ACME-4589).
3. Propose locking in a new multi-year term to restore a volume/term
   discount, ideally before the account drifts further on month-to-month
   pricing.
