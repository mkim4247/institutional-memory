# Acme Corp — New Support Ticket

## Ticket #ACME-4589 (Open)
**Opened:** 2026-07-22
**Priority:** P2
**Reported by:** Dave Ortiz (Director of IT Operations)

**Summary:** Acme is seeing elevated latency (roughly 2-3x baseline) on the
analytics add-on during their end-of-month reporting runs for the past two
cycles. They suspect it's related to the increased data volume from a new
business unit that came online in Q2.

**Current status:** Our infra team is investigating. Preliminary finding
points to Acme approaching the soft usage ceiling for their current tier of
the analytics add-on, not a platform bug.

**Impact to renewal conversation:** This ticket should be factored into the
renewal proposal — Acme may need a higher-capacity analytics tier, which is
a pricing conversation, not just a support fix. Framing this as a growth
story ("you've outgrown your current tier") is likely to land better than
framing it as an unresolved complaint.

**Note:** This ticket was opened AFTER the account history and ticket log
in `acme-support-ticket-log.md` (round 1), which described the account as
